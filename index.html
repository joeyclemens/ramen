Method

Tap a step to tick it off as you cook. Steps adjust to your order.

  1. Build the broth

    Simmer the stock with the onion, garlic and ginger. Strain and discard the solids.

    15–20 min

  2. Season it

    Whisk in the miso, tahini, soy and sesame oil. Keep it below a rolling boil so the miso stays bright. Taste, adjust, keep warm.

    2 min

  3. Soft eggs

    Boil for 6½ minutes, cool in cold water, peel and halve.

    7 min

  4. Chicken Sear it

    Fry the thighs in oil, 5–6 minutes a side, until deeply golden. Rest, then slice.

    12 min

  5. Quorn Crisp it

    Use a separate pan so the veggie bowls stay meat-free. Fry in oil for 6–8 minutes until golden at the edges.

    8 min

  6. Noodles & veg

    Cook the noodles to the packet instructions. For the last minute, add the pak choi, sweetcorn and carrot to the same water. Drain well and divide between bowls.

    4 min

  7. Build the bowls

    Ladle over the hot broth. Add the egg and spring onions, then the chicken or Quorn on top.

Memo
  • Soupier? Add stock but keep the miso and soy as written.
  • Quorn is milder than chicken. A splash of soy or a spoon of gochujang in its bowls helps.
  • Quorn usually contains egg white, and some ramen noodles contain egg. Check packets for strict vegans.
  • Chilli oil on the side for anyone who wants heat.
